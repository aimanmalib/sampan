# 0081: Cutting the demo video

Date: 2026-11-20

## Context

The raw clips of ADR 0079 have to become one video of two to three minutes with a voice, subtitles and the
numbers of `docs/MEASURED.md`. The recorded swap and the first conversion of the recorded Bring in were dropped by
the network (ADR 0080), so the edit must not show them.

## Decision

- `tools/video/edit.sh` (steps in `tools/video/edit.ts`, pure parts in `tools/video/plan.ts`) cuts the video with
  ffmpeg. Output paths are arguments.
- Scenes, words and clip windows live in `tools/video/captions.json`. A window is a mark of `state/video/shots.json`
  plus an offset in seconds. Captions take their numbers from placeholders: `{headline}` comes from the same counter
  as the README bounty table, and `{<scene>.seconds}` comes from the ok Runs row holding the scene's transaction. An
  unknown placeholder stops the edit.
- A shot marked `ok: false` is refused unless the clip names the mark where the dropped transaction appears
  (`endsBefore`) and the window ends before it. The swap was recorded again (`bac74ab7…`). The Bring in clip stops
  at the crossing receipt. A new `receipt` scene of `tools/video/record.ts` then opens the landing status page again
  on the same built server and database, and it shows the conversion the network kept (`f30306b8…`).
- Each scene lasts as long as its clips as recorded, with no speed-up, and never shorter than its narration. Waits are
  cut, and the clock of ADR 0079 shows the jump.
- The narration is an ElevenLabs stock voice ("Alice", `eleven_multilingual_v2`). It is declared on the end card
  and in the README. Each line is cached by its text. The key is read from `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` or a key file given as
  an argument and is only sent as a request header.
- The music is a quiet sustained chord generated by ffmpeg in the same run. It is original, so there is no
  licence to track.
- Loudness is normalised in two passes to -16 LUFS with a -1.5 dBTP ceiling. English subtitles are burned in and
  also written as SRT and WebVTT. The tool measures the result: length, audio length, loudness, true peak and
  silences in the first 15 s. It also saves frames at 5, 30 and 60 s, at full size and at 390 px wide.

## Consequences

- Changing the words or windows means editing `captions.json` and running the edit again. Only changed lines call
  the voice API again.
- To go back to no voice, run `--no-voice`: subtitles and music only.
